## Overview

This portfolio serves as a platform to present my background in Computer Engineering, my proficiency in data analysis tools, and my commitment to solving real-world problems through data-driven solutions. The live version of the site can be accessed at [vion30.github.io/Portfolio-Website/](https://vion30.github.io/Portfolio-Website/).

## Features

- **Home Section:** Introduction and professional title.
- **About Section:** Detailed information about my background, skills, and expertise.
- **Resume Section:** Overview of my education and professional experience.
- **Projects Section:** Highlights of selected data analytics projects with descriptions and links.
- **Contact Section:** Information on how to reach me, including address, phone number, and email.
